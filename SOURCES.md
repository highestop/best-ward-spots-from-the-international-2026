## Repository asset provenance

- The 50 core screenshots were downloaded from the Miro board's public original-image resources at 1920 × 1080.
- Their order was matched against the author's Radiant/Dire source deck and cross-checked against the Combined deck.
- The 36 extra screenshots were extracted from slides 2–37 of the author's Extra Wards From TI deck at 1920 × 1080.
- Images are stored locally as WebP at quality 86. No guide image is loaded from Miro, Google Slides, YouTube, or a temporary transfer service at display time.
- Display labels normalize obvious filename spelling errors. A dagger (`†`) marks a maintainer-written label where the Miro upload was only named `image.png`.
- Tactical statements in [video-guide.md](docs/video-guide.md) are condensed from the video's English narration. The repository does not present those summaries as verbatim quotations.

## Known source inconsistency

The Miro board labels the third Radiant lane group as **BOT**. Slide 21 of the Radiant Dire TI Wards deck displays **TOP** in the corresponding position. This guide follows the Miro board and uses **Bottom**.
