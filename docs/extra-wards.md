# Extra ward spots

These 36 screenshots come from slides 2–37 of RoJack's **Extra Wards From TI** deck, linked from the original Miro board. The source deck does not assign individual location names, so `X-01` through `X-36` preserve its slide order without adding speculative labels.

Two slides contain explicit source notes:

- **X-01:** “Need to cut the tree first.”
- **X-05:** “Don't put this in the early game. It's meant for mid to late game.”
